Four zones, taught in sequence

Two ways in

The curriculum is the same either way. What differs is who arranges it, how long you spend, and how close the guidance gets.

Route One
For colleges

We come to your campus and teach a full batch. Arranged through the entrepreneurship cell, paid for by the institution.

  • Campus seminar — 2 hours, whole batch
  • Pharma-tech workshop — 1–2 days, hands-on
  • Industry partnership — discussed case by case
Request a Proposal

Route Two
For doctors and students

Join a small cohort yourself. Eight to twelve weeks of direct mentorship through all four zones, with the same mentors throughout.

  • Founder track — 8–12 week cohort
  • Small group, so questions get answered
  • Ends at Swarna, the full certification rung

Taught by people who still do the work

The academy is led by Dr. Vikas Saroch and Dr. Neetika Saroch, both MD (Ayurveda), and taught by a cadre of mentors drawn from clinical practice and licensed manufacturing. Between practice and the technical direction of a GMP-certified unit, the material taught here is the material used in production — which is why the sessions are demonstrations rather than lectures.

HHAP, the unit behind the teaching, holds AYUSH manufacturing licence HP-301-Ay and operates from Kangra, Himachal Pradesh.
