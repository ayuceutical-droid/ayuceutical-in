Ayurvedic pharma-tech mentorship · India

You can recite the classics. Can you make the medicine?

Ayuceutical Academy is a mentorship programme in Rasashastra and Ayurvedic pharmaceutics — authentication, preparation, licensing and quality law. Taught by MD specialists who run a GMP-certified manufacturing unit, so what you learn is what production actually requires.

Colleges bring it to campus for a full batch. Individual doctors and students join a cohort directly.

The certification ladder

Named for the metals of Rasashastra. Each zone completed moves a student one rung.

TamraCopper — one zone
KansyaBronze — two zones
RajataSilver — three zones
SwarnaGold — all four, with certificate of completion

Four zones, taught in sequence

Each zone is a working session with materials in hand. Students authenticate, prepare, document and defend — in that order, because that is the order the work happens in.

01

Authenticate

Raw drug identity

Organoleptic and physical identification of herbs, minerals and metals. What adulteration looks like in a real consignment, and how a purchase decision is defended on paper.

02

Make

Classical pharmaceutics

Shodhana, marana and the classical dosage forms, demonstrated end to end. Students handle the apparatus and record the process the way a production file requires.

03

Licence to launch

Setting up lawfully

What an AYUSH manufacturing licence actually requires, what GMP inspection looks for, and the sequence a graduate follows to go from intention to a licensed unit.

04

Quality and law

Testing and compliance

Drug Testing Laboratory protocols, specification writing, and the advertising and labelling rules that govern every Ayurvedic product sold in India.

Two ways in

The curriculum is the same either way. What differs is who arranges it, how long you spend, and how close the guidance gets.

  • Industry partnership — discussed case by case

Taught by people who still do the work

The academy is led by Dr. Vikas Saroch and Dr. Neetika Kundailia Saroch, both MD (Ayurveda), and taught by a cadre of mentors drawn from clinical practice and licensed manufacturing. Between practice and the technical direction of a GMP-certified unit, the material taught here is the material used in production — which is why the sessions are demonstrations rather than lectures.

Himalayan Heal Ayurvedic Pharmacy, the unit behind the teaching, holds AYUSH manufacturing licence HP-301-Ay and operates from Kangra, Himachal Pradesh.
